Rules the board checks

Workload capacity

Each teacher has weekly hours available. Going over is flagged in red before you submit.

Specialization

Subjects outside a teacher's declared areas are marked so the HOD can add a reason.

Preparations

More than 4 different subjects for one teacher is flagged, since each needs separate preparation.

Full coverage

Every subject-section needs a teacher. Unassigned ones stay visible until covered.

How allocation runs

  1. Collect inputs. Subjects with credits and weekly hours, plus faculty with designation, specialization and capacity.
  2. Place core subjects first. Match high-credit subjects to specialists and senior faculty.
  3. Add labs and electives. Assign lab batches and fill the remaining hours while balancing load.
  4. Review. Resolve flags, then send the allocation to the HOD and Principal for approval.
FacultyFit is a demo of a faculty subject allocation workflow. Sample names and hours are fictional.
